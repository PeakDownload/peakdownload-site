Game IQ: Basketball Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Overview

Game IQ: Basketball does not collect personal information. The app does not require an account and does not transmit learning progress, preferences, or usage data to Peak Download LLC.

Data stored on your device

Learning progress and settings are stored locally on your device. Peak Download does not receive or maintain a remote copy of that local app data.

Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Game IQ: Basketball does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, crash reporting, or third-party data-collection SDKs.

App-store records

Peak Download does not receive purchase or entitlement status from the app. Apple App Store and Google Play may separately maintain download or transaction records under their own privacy policies.

Children and families

Game IQ: Basketball does not collect personal information from children or other users. Parents or guardians may contact us with privacy questions.

Retention and deletion

Deleting the app or clearing its local app storage removes locally stored learning progress and settings from that device. Peak Download normally has no remote copy of that local data to delete. See Game IQ data deletion instructions for more information.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to privacy@peakdownload.com.
