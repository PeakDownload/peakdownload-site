HomeOps Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Overview

This policy describes how Peak Download LLC handles information for HomeOps, a home systems, maintenance, records, and planning product.

Information we may collect

  • Home, system, appliance, maintenance, reminder, note, and record information entered by the user.
  • Documents or images the user chooses to attach, where supported.
  • Subscription or entitlement status handled through the applicable app store.
  • Usage information about how you interact with HomeOps features (for example, which setup steps you complete, when tasks are marked done, or when a document is added), associated with a persistent, anonymous identifier tied to your device rather than your name or email, used to improve reliability and understand how the app is used.

HomeOps is a mobile app and does not use browser cookies. The usage information above is collected through analytics tools built into the app itself.

How we use information

We use information to operate HomeOps, provide support, improve reliability, and respond to requests.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal information.
  • We do not use app data for third-party advertising resale.
  • We do not collect more information than is reasonably needed for product operation and support.

Service providers

HomeOps may rely on platform services, cloud hosting, payment processing, analytics, and crash reporting where needed to operate and improve the product.

Retention and deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for product operation, support, legal, or business purposes. See HomeOps account and data deletion instructions to delete your account in the app or request deletion without the app.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests may be sent to privacy@peakdownload.com.
