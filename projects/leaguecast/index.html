FAQ

LeagueCast questions.

What is LeagueCast?

LeagueCast is a live recreational league platform for scores, schedules, matchups, standings, player results, and league-night coverage.

Does LeagueCast require a particular bowling center or scoring system?

No. LeagueCast is designed around the league, not the facility. Commissioners and scorekeepers can enter results directly, so your league can keep using LeagueCast when teams bowl at different centers or the center's scoring system is not connected.

Is LeagueCast a personal bowling-statistics app?

LeagueCast includes player and team stats, but its main job is making league night feel alive: live matchups, standings, races, results, season records, and the story of your league.

Is LeagueCast only for bowling?

Bowling is the first supported sport. Other recreational league sports are future opportunities, not available features today.

Can LeagueCast support our scoring format?

LeagueCast supports structured, league-specific scoring rather than forcing every league into one generic model. Contact PeakDownload to evaluate custom scoring options for your league.

Can teams compete from different locations?

Yes. Teams can bowl at different locations while scores, matchups, and standings remain synchronized.

What does LeagueCast cost?

LeagueCast is free at launch.

When will LeagueCast be available?

LeagueCast is live now on the App Store for iOS. The Android app is in Google Play review and will be available soon.
