Account deletion

Delete your LeagueCast account and data

LeagueCast is published by Peak Download LLC. You can delete your account in the app or request help without installing the app.

Delete your account in LeagueCast

If you have not yet created or joined a league:

  1. Open LeagueCast and sign in.
  2. On the entry screen, tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete Account and confirm the deletion when prompted.

If you already belong to a league:

  1. Open LeagueCast and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Account & Privacy.
  4. Tap Delete Account and confirm the deletion when prompted.

Request deletion without the app

Email contact@peakdownload.com with the subject "LeagueCast Account Deletion Request." If possible, send the request from the email address associated with your LeagueCast account and identify the app as LeagueCast. Do not send your password. We may need to verify that you control the account before processing the request.

What LeagueCast deletes

Confirming deletion removes your LeagueCast sign-in immediately. LeagueCast then removes:

  • Your account profile.
  • Your membership records in any leagues, teams, and seasons you participated in.

League and season records that other participants rely on, such as scores and standings, are preserved as part of the league's history and are not deleted.

Processing time

Your sign-in is removed as soon as you confirm the request. Associated data cleanup normally completes at the same time; if it does not, it is retried automatically. We do not commit to a fixed completion time. Requests submitted by email may take longer while we verify the request.
